The first release of Axo: a calm buddy in your MacBook notch for posture and breaks.

- **Posture checks.** The camera looks for 3 seconds each minute. Pictures are checked on your Mac and never sent. AirPods head motion helps when the camera is off.
- **Breaks that fit your day.** Stand up, rest your eyes, stretch and drink water. Reminders wait while you are in a call, away or on a locked screen.
- **16 buddies, plus secret buddies that open with a code.** Pick one, give it a name and a color. It grows as you look after yourself.
- **A calm voice coach** guides each stretch. It stays quiet in calls.
- **Progress.** A daily score, streaks, goals and levels, all kept on your Mac.

Needs macOS 14 or later. Free, with no account.
